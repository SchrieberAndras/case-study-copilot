# AI-sounding text: what to flag

Professors increasingly notice AI-generated wording, and it costs credibility even when the analysis is good. Check every slide, the speaker notes and the Q&A file. Flag each instance with slide number, the exact phrase, and a plainer rewrite. Don't rewrite in the deck unless asked.

If the `humanizer` skill is available, read its SKILL.md for the full pattern list; this file is the short version.

## Word-level tells
- Inflated vocabulary: leverage, robust, seamless, holistic, synergy, unlock, empower, elevate, foster, navigate, landscape, realm, tapestry, delve, pivotal, crucial, vital, paramount, cutting-edge, game-changer, transformative, innovative (without saying how), journey, ecosystem (outside tech).
- Vague intensifiers: significantly, substantially, truly, deeply, incredibly, effectively (with no number behind them).
- Empty corporate phrasing: "drive meaningful impact", "create value", "enhance the overall experience", "strategic initiatives", "key stakeholders".

## Structure tells
- Rule of three everywhere: three adjectives, three parallel bullets on every slide.
- "Not just X, but Y" / "It's not about X, it's about Y" constructions.
- Superficial -ing endings: "..., ensuring long-term growth", "..., highlighting the importance of ...".
- Em dashes used as the default connector, several per slide.
- Sentences that restate the title in different words.
- Every bullet the same length and rhythm.
- Grand openers or closers: "In today's fast-paced world", "In conclusion", "This positions X for sustainable success".

## Content tells
- Claims without numbers or case evidence.
- Generic recommendations that would fit any company ("invest in digital transformation", "enhance customer experience").
- Over-hedging or balanced-sounding filler ("while there are challenges, there are also opportunities").
- Invented-sounding precision (a statistic with no source).

## How to report
`Slide N – "leverage synergies to unlock holistic value" – AI tell (inflated vocabulary, no content) – rewrite: "share one supplier contract across both brands, saving ~$2M/yr"`

Give a per-deck verdict: Low / Medium / High risk of reading as AI-written, with the 3 worst slides.
