# Framework menu

Run what plausibly applies; show only what produces an insight. For each shown framework: findings → "so what" for the decision.

## Framing (always)
- **Issue tree (MECE)**: split the case question into non-overlapping sub-questions. Use it to plan the analysis and to check nothing is missed.
- **Hypothesis-driven approach**: state the likely answer, list what must be true for it to hold, test each.
- **Stakeholder map**: who decides, who is affected, whose support is needed (owners, employees, operators, customers, regulators, activists).

## Company
- **Business model canvas / value proposition**: how it makes money, for whom, why customers choose it.
- **Unit economics**: revenue per unit/store/customer, cost structure, margins, payback. Use case exhibits and endnotes.
- **Financial baseline (always first)**: yearly revenue, yearly profit, and cash, debt and net debt, from the income statement, balance sheet and statement of cash flows. Procedure, private-company fallback and the table are in `financial-planning.md`.
- **Financial ratios / growth**: CAGR, growth slowing or accelerating, revenue vs systemwide sales.
- **Value chain**: which activities create the advantage.
- **VRIO**: which resources are a sustained advantage (valuable, rare, inimitable, organised) vs eroding.
- **7S (McKinsey)**: when the problem is culture, structure, or scaling an organisation.
- **SWOT**: only as a summary of other findings, never as the main analysis.

## Environment (course order: macro, then micro)
- **PESTEL**: macro drivers (inflation, regulation, social trends, tech, environment).
- **Porter's Five Forces**: industry attractiveness and pressure on margins.
- **Competitor benchmark**: 4-6 rivals on the dimensions that matter (Harvey balls).
- **Customer segmentation / jobs-to-be-done**: who buys, why, and what they are hiring the product for. Essential for positioning cases.
- **Strategic group map / perceptual map**: positioning versus rivals on two axes (e.g. price vs quality).
- **Trend scan**: market and technology trends with evidence (useful when the brief asks for "market trends").
- **Key industry drivers + competitive landscape**: 3-4 drivers, then a 2x2 of the two decisive dimensions with competitors placed and the **target dimension** the client should move into.
- **STP with personas**: segment, target, position; 3-4 personas with key attributes, pain points and how to reach them.
- **Customer journey (pain points)**: the stages of the process, each marked well executed / wrong or missing / dependent on earlier steps; shows which stages to fix.
- **Personas + journey vs competitor**: satisfaction per stage for each persona, client vs competitor; shows where the competitor wins.
- **Adoption curve**: innovators 2.5%, early adopters 13.5%, early majority 34%, late majority 34%, laggards 16%, with a persona per target group. Only for new product or technology adoption cases.

Organise the shown findings as macro environment → micro environment → internal abilities, and end the analysis in one main challenge or opportunity.

## Problem
- **Root cause (5 whys / fishbone)**: separate symptoms (falling scores, complaints) from causes.
- **Gap analysis**: where the company is vs where it needs to be, per dimension.

## Strategy generation
- **Decision tree**: the strategic options branching 2-3 levels deep, each kept or killed with a one-line reason; shown on the Strategy main slide.
- **Profit / issue tree (MECE)**: the target metric split into revenue and cost levers down to concrete levers; used to brainstorm options.
- **Ansoff matrix**: market penetration, market development, product development, diversification.
- **Porter's generic strategies**: cost, differentiation, focus – and the risk of being stuck in the middle.
- **Blue Ocean (ERRC grid)**: eliminate, reduce, raise, create.
- **Build / buy / partner**: how to execute a capability gap.
- **Scenario analysis**: best / base / worst, or 2x2 of key uncertainties.

## Feasibility and impact
- **Affordability vs baseline**: investment against cash, yearly cash flow and debt headroom (`financial-planning.md` section 5); shown first on the financial slide.
- **Financial breakdown**: bottom-up (cost drivers, main assumptions, cash flow, breakeven) or top-down (WACC, growth, results by segment).
- **Hard and soft KPIs**: financial and volume numbers; non-financial impacts.
- **Balanced Scorecard strategy map**: objectives per perspective with KPI, target and initiative (appendix).
- **Risk matrix**: probability x impact, one mitigation each.
- **Gantt / 3-phase roadmap**: quick wins, build, scale.

## Picking guide by case type
- Growth vs culture / scaling: 7S, VRIO, value chain, root cause, stakeholder map.
- Positioning / category confusion: STP with personas, JTBD, customer journey, perceptual map, Porter generic strategies, competitor benchmark.
- Digital product / app / customer experience: personas, customer journey vs competitor, adoption curve, best-practice use cases.
- Sustainability / ESG + digital: PESTEL, trend scan, stakeholder map, barriers analysis, Balanced Scorecard metrics.
- Market entry / expansion: Ansoff, Five Forces, PESTEL, unit economics.
