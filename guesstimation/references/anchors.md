## Useful heuristics
- **Geometric mean** for a range you can only bound: between 10 and 1,000 -> 100.
- **Rule of 72**: years to double = 72 / growth % per year.
- **Replacement cycle**: annual sales = installed base / lifetime in years.
- **Per-capita sense check**: divide the answer by population and ask whether that is believable behaviour.
